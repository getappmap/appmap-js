Old site vs. new site: the link map

A plain-English guide to what's new, what's staying, what's being kept as-is, and what's going away — so you can see the whole picture at a glance.

Old site: live appmap.io (180 pages) New site: Lovable rebuild (18 pages) Prepared for: Kevin

The big picture in four sentences

The marketing pages (home, pricing, how it works, and so on) have been rebuilt fresh on the new site.

The Docs and the Blog were not rebuilt — all ~165 of those pages still run on the old website, and the new site simply points to them. Visitors won't notice a seam; behind the scenes it's still the old system serving those pages.

A handful of brand-new pages exist that the old site never had (Benchmarks, Enterprise, Architecture, and more).

And a small set of old pages are going away — mostly ones that got folded into a new page — which means we should set up redirects so their old links don't break.

Decisions for Kevin

The open questions worth a quick call before launch.

  • Set up the redirects. At least: /download, /contact, /contact/sales, /company/about-appmap, and the two /product/… pages. Without these, old links and Google results will 404.
  • Decide the fate of four orphan pages. Brand Assets, Code of Conduct, the Feedback form, and Blog Gallery have no new home. Keep on the old site, rebuild, or retire?
  • Confirm Docs & Blog stay on the old system. That's ~165 pages still served by the old website behind the new one. Is that the intended long-term plan, or a temporary bridge?
  • Confirm the Navie docs redirect. Old /docs/navie/… links now send people to the new "How it works" page instead of the detailed Navie documentation. Intended?
  • Confirm the dropped social & careers links. X, Slack, YouTube, and Careers are gone from the footer. On purpose, or should any come back?
  • Confirm the booking link. Demo booking moved from HubSpot to a Google Calendar link. Is Google Calendar the one we want?
